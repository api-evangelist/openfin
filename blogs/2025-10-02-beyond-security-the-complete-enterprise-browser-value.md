Enterprise browsers have gained significant attention as organizations recognize the security limitations of consumer browsers in business environments. Recent vendor discussions focus heavily on security features, data loss prevention, threat isolation, compliance controls. These are important questions, but they represent only one dimension of what enterprise browsers should deliver.
