Traditional web browsers were designed for consumers to search the public internet, but much of what employees are searching for lives in their work apps: a stock in Bloomberg Terminal, an email in Microsoft Outlook, or a client account in Salesforce. What employees need is a unified way to search for specific information across all of their apps simultaneously. HERE Enterprise Browser does exactly that—and so much more.
